Referral finder and reply-time line on the contacts card

Referral finder ("Who could refer you" on each job page)
- Alumni: employees at the company who went to a college on your profile (college names are
  compared by their distinguishing words, so "PSG College of Technology" and "PSG Tech" agree),
  most recent graduates first, each with a draft message you can copy.
- People you know: add someone you know at the company (name and how you know them); only you can
  see them; draft a message for them too. Up to 10 per company.
- Drafts (lib/referral.ts) are templates built from your profile and the job, claim only skills you
  actually have, ask for a short chat before a referral, and are never sent. There are no contact
  details for colleagues on this site, by design.
- Migration 1200: company_employees (readable when signed in, fictional demo data) and connections
  (owner-only RLS, relationship limited to five kinds). SQL checks cover privacy and anon access.

Reply-time line
- The recruiter and founder card on the job page now shows how fast the company replies, from real
  application histories on the site, with the small-sample caveat.

Tests: 7 new unit tests (259 total), SQL checks, headless run: signed-out prompt, alumni match and
exclusion, draft content, add and remove a connection, no-alumni message, reply-time line, no console
errors.

Not handled: matching only knows the colleges and employees in this database (no real social graph
or LinkedIn import); college aliases beyond common words (for example "NIT Trichy" versus the full
name) will not match; the employees are fictional.
