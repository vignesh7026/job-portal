Salary negotiator for offered applications

- When an application reaches Offered, its page shows "You have an offer": a market range (25th to
  75th percentile and median) computed from other listings on this site with the same level and job
  type (preferring ones that share a skill), and an editable draft reply.
- lib/negotiate.ts: pure percentile, midpoint and formatting maths plus the plain fallback draft,
  which never invents a number. The range needs 3+ comparable listings, otherwise it says so.
- New AI kind "negotiate" on /api/ai/[kind]: owner only, 422 unless the status is Offered. The prompt
  may use only the figures supplied (the candidate's own offer, the range). Cached per user,
  application, offer text and range. Falls back to the template if the model is down.
- The range is labelled as a hint about this site, not the whole market. Nothing is sent.

Tests: 8 new unit tests (198 total); headless run on an offered application: card, range, draft
signed with the first name, no console errors.

Not handled: salaries on listings are ranges set by the employer, not what people were paid; the
candidate's current salary and location are not factored in.
