Home page product demo (stageshow-style miniature of the app)

- components/home/product-demo.tsx: a framed miniature built from the app's own parts (match ring,
  status badge, company mark) and tokens. About 12 seconds, once, when it scrolls into view:
  search -> results -> cursor picks the best match -> its page opens -> Apply -> sending -> applied
  -> status timeline fills to Shortlisted. The cursor only moves to where the next thing happens
  and fades out when nothing is left to do. Ends on that last frame, which is also the poster.
- Reduced motion and first paint show the poster; no autoplay. Replay button for everyone else.
- Decorative only: aria-hidden and inert, with a visible caption; no extra primary button.
- Home layout: headline and search on the left, demo on the right at lg, stacked below.
- This replaces the planned React Three Fiber hero, so no 3D dependencies are added.

Tests: tsc, eslint, unit pass; headless check of the full sequence, final state, inert, no
horizontal scroll, no console errors.

Not handled: the script timing is fixed (not tied to real data); very narrow phones show the
demo below the fold and the cursor positions are measured once per beat, not on resize.
